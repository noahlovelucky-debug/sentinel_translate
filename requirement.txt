Sentinel Translate V3.2 local requirements
Last updated: 2026-08-06

Training data (no validation/test leakage)
  /data/sentinel_translate/data/shards_v2/train/index.json
  split must be train; high-frequency samples must identify year 2017 or 2018.
  delta_days must be 0 or 1 and the registration/cloud/validity audit must pass.

Validation and closed-test data
  /data/sentinel_translate/data/manifests/pairs.jsonl
  raw rasters referenced by the manifest under /data/data_disk/data_dir
  validation_temporal must contain exactly 463 pairs.

Compatible initialization weights
  /data/code/sentinel_translate_v3_1/checkpoints/physical/step_*.pt
  These may be loaded with --init-model. Their optimizer, scheduler, RNG, and sampler state
  must never be restored into V3.2.

V1/V2 physical baselines
  /data/sentinel_translate/checkpoints/sar2s2/mean/best.pt
  /data/sentinel_translate/checkpoints_v2/sar2s2/refiner/best.pt

Mandatory Visual evaluation dependencies
  lpips>=0.1.4
  DISTS-pytorch>=0.1 with package weights.pt
  AlexNet/VGG weights installed by scripts/download_eval_weights.sh
  Missing dependencies are fatal for Visual validation.

Output isolation
  checkpoints: /data/code/sentinel_translat/v3.2/checkpoints_v32
  reports:     /data/code/sentinel_translat/v3.2/reports_v32

Research-only comparison
  DiffusionSat ControlNet/public 512x512 checkpoint is not a training dependency.
  Do not redistribute it or merge it into any V3.2 checkpoint. Record that its license is
  undeclared when reporting the comparison.
