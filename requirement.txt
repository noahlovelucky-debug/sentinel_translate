Sentinel Translate V3.1: data and weight requirements
Last updated: 2026-08-06

1. Current Sentinel V3.1 experiment: required data

No additional remote-sensing dataset download is required to finish the current
Sentinel-only V3.1 training run. The required assets already exist locally.

Training shards
  Path: /data/sentinel_translate/data/shards_v2/train/index.json
  Payload: paired Sentinel-1 VV/VH and Sentinel-2 ten-band patches, validity
  masks, acquisition/orbit metadata, and the 10/20/40 m synthetic-GSD views
  generated by the loader.
  Size: about 61 GB.
  Required for: all V3.1 training stages.

Pair manifest and raw evaluation rasters
  Manifest: /data/sentinel_translate/data/manifests/pairs.jsonl
  Raw scene root referenced by the manifest: /data/data_disk/data_dir
  Payload: Sentinel-2 B02/B03/B04, red-edge, NIR and SWIR GeoTIFFs; Sentinel-1
  VV/VH GeoTIFFs; and Sentinel-2 SCL masks.
  Required for: closed validation/test reports and fixed visual panels.
  Note: the manifest paths must remain valid. The shards alone are sufficient
  for training but not for the closed-split evaluator.

Expected closed splits in the manifest
  validation_temporal: 2019 model selection
  test_temporal:       2020 future-time test
  test_spatial:        2019 spatial holdout
  test_joint:          2020 spatial plus future-time holdout

2. Current experiment: required checkpoints and weights

V3 initialization checkpoint
  Path: /data/code/sentinel_translate_v3/checkpoints/pretrain/step_0008000.pt
  Size: about 2.5 GB.
  Required only for a brand-new V3.1 run. It initializes the shared V3 scene
  representation. It is not needed when resuming the current V3.1 run.

V3.1 resumable checkpoints
  Root: /data/code/sentinel_translate_v3_1/checkpoints
  Required for: interruption recovery, final model selection, and evaluation.
  Each checkpoint contains model, EMA, optimizer, scheduler, RNG, and sampler
  state. Do not download or substitute unrelated weights.

No external foundation-model weights are used for training.
  The V3.1 scene encoder and residual DiT are trained from the migrated V3
  checkpoint, not from ImageNet, CLIP, SAM, or a diffusion foundation model.

3. Optional evaluation weights

These are not required to train or to compute RMSE, SAM, Edge F1, PSD, ENL,
histogram, uncertainty, or retrieval metrics. They are required only to report
LPIPS and DISTS for the optical visual branch.

LPIPS AlexNet and VGG-16 ImageNet weights
  Script: /data/code/sentinel_translate_v3_1/scripts/download_eval_weights.sh
  Cache: ${TORCH_HOME:-$HOME/.cache/torch}/hub/checkpoints
  Files downloaded and SHA-prefix checked by the script:
    alexnet-owt-7be5be79.pth
    vgg16-397923af.pth
  Python package: lpips>=0.1.4

DISTS weights
  Python package: DISTS-pytorch>=0.1
  The package supplies weights.pt; no separate manual download is expected.

Install optional perceptual evaluation dependencies:
  pip install -e '/data/code/sentinel_translate_v3_1[eval]'
  bash /data/code/sentinel_translate_v3_1/scripts/download_eval_weights.sh

4. Data required before claiming multi-sensor transfer (not yet supported by
   the current data driver or training config)

These are future expansion requirements, not prerequisites for the Sentinel
V3.1 result. Each must be paired in space/time, radiometrically audited, and
added through a new SensorSpec plus data driver before use.

Landsat / HLS optical data
  Purpose: verify real 30 m optical spectral-response and GSD transfer.
  Recommended products: NASA HLS L30/S30 surface reflectance and/or USGS
  Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 surface reflectance.
  Access: NASA Earthdata login for HLS; USGS EarthExplorer or cloud catalog
  for Landsat Collection 2.
  Official references:
    https://hls.gsfc.nasa.gov/data-access-and-tools/
    https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2

SpaceNet 6 SAR plus optical data
  Purpose: test high-resolution X-band SAR-to-optical transfer with a public
  benchmark. It is not a drop-in paired reflectance training set; check its
  labels, acquisition geometry, optical source, and license before creating
  pairs.
  Reference: https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.06500

Capella / Umbra SAR imagery
  Purpose: commercial high-resolution X-band SAR domain transfer.
  Requirement: a paid or research data agreement and permission to retain,
  process, and publish derived benchmark results. Do not plan this as an open
  download.

NISAR L-band SAR products
  Purpose: validate true L-band transfer and polarization/incidence-angle
  conditioning.
  Access: NASA Earthdata / ASF DAAC. Obtain the product specification before
  selecting a product level and pairing it with optical observations.
  Official reference: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/data/

5. Minimum metadata required for every future sensor driver

  Sensor and product identifier; acquisition UTC; CRS and geolocation;
  native GSD and storage-grid GSD; wavelength/frequency; polarization; orbit
  and incidence angle when SAR; radiometric unit and calibration; nodata/cloud
  masks; processing level; license; and an immutable source scene identifier.

6. Deliberate exclusions

  Do not use 2021-2022 Sentinel directories as a test set until their
  completeness, calibration, pairing, and split leakage audit is complete.
  Do not treat monthly, quarterly, or annual composites as high-frequency
  image-to-image ground truth.
